muon
Multimodal omics data standard and analysis framework
with Ilia Kats

Experimental designs grow in scale and complexity, and multiple layers of information are now profiled in individual cells. Such advances enable new data-driven insights and also pose significant challenges to handling, analysing and interpreting the data.

We designed a data standard (MuData) to address the need for a data management solution for multimodal datasets. We also built a multimodal analysis framework (muon) around it in order to handle, process, integrate and visualise complex multimodal datasets. Importantly, the data standard and the framework are integrated into a larger ecosystem of analysis tools for high-throughput omics and build the foundation for its further development and expansion.
